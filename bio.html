My name is Corin White! I live in a house on a street with other people nearby. I love programming and game dev as well as making mods for games, specifically Minecraft.

I like making survival games, puzzle games and platformer games. My favourite game to mod is Minecraft because it is easy to change textures and add aspects into the game. I love messing around with different limitations and concepts. For example, I made 2 mods called Mine and Craft.

About My Mods:

Mine:

In this mod, you can no longer craft. All recipies are disabled, so is yor inventory crafting. If a crafting table or a crafter is in your inventory, they will be deleted.

Craft:

In this mod, you can no longer mine. All mining tools become useless but you can still craft. Explosions still blow things up and you will be given a crafting table whenever you load into a world.
